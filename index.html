Privacy Policy for Turbo Track

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Introduction

Turbo Track respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Turbo Track mobile game.

Information Collection

Turbo Track does not intentionally collect, store, or share personal information from users through core gameplay.

Third-Party Services and Advertising SDK

Turbo Track includes a third-party advertising SDK integrated into the application package. However, this SDK is currently inactive and unused in the current version of the game and does not collect or transmit personal data at this time. We may activate or integrate advertising services in future updates to support the game, and this privacy policy will be updated accordingly prior to any data collection.

Children's Privacy

Turbo Track does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future if the game changes, including when advertising features or third-party services are activated. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the Google Play store listing for Turbo Track.
